SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 15 December 2026 between Nimbus Software Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated in India with its registered office at 21 MG Road, Bengaluru 560001 ("Service Provider"), and Orchid Retail Private Limited, a company incorporated in India with its registered office at 8 Link Road, Mumbai 400053 ("Client").

1. Parties
1.1 This Agreement is between Nimbus Software Pvt. Ltd. ("Service Provider") and Orchid Retail Private Limited ("Client"). Each is a "Party" and together they are the "Parties".

2. Effective Date and Term
2.1 This Agreement starts on 1 January 2027 (the "Effective Date") and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months, ending on 31 December 2027, unless ended earlier under this Agreement.

3. Services
3.1 The Service Provider will provide the Client with a hosted inventory management platform and related support services as described in Schedule A.
3.2 The Service Provider will make the platform available at least 99 percent of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance.

4. Payment Terms
4.1 The Client shall pay the Service Provider a monthly fee of INR 2,50,000 plus applicable taxes. The Service Provider will invoice the Client on the first day of each month.
4.2 The Client shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late payments will bear interest at one and a half percent (1.5%) per month until paid in full.

5. Termination
5.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party thirty (30) days written notice.
5.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within fifteen (15) days of receiving written notice of the breach.

6. Limitation of Liability
6.1 Each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Client to the Service Provider in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
6.2 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential loss, including loss of profit or loss of data.

7. Confidentiality
7.1 Each Party shall keep the other Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.
7.2 This obligation of confidentiality continues for a period of three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

8. Governing Law
8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts located in Mumbai, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

9. Automatic Renewal
9.1 At the end of the initial term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months each, unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

10. Notices
10.1 All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by email with a copy by courier to the address of the receiving Party stated at the top of this Agreement.

11. Entire Agreement
11.1 This Agreement, together with Schedule A, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and replaces all earlier discussions and understandings.
